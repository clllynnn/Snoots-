# Snoots! Maps MVP

## Product Focus

Maps is Snoots!’s entry point. Its immediate promise is:

> Can I take my dog here, confidently?

The MVP centers on one high-value flow:

```text
Search nearby → understand the exact dog policy → decide → navigate → confirm or update after visiting
```

## MVP: Explore Map

- Open on the user’s current area.
- Display nearby pet-friendly venues as map pins and in a scrollable result list.
- Support categories: café, restaurant, park, hotel, grooming, pet shop, and veterinary clinic.
- Provide first-pass filters:
  - Open now
  - Indoor allowed
  - Outdoor only
  - Small dogs allowed
  - Large dogs allowed
  - Stroller or carrier required
  - Verified information

## MVP: Venue Details

Every venue needs a clear policy card rather than an ambiguous “pet-friendly” label.

- Indoor and outdoor access
- Size or weight restrictions
- Required equipment: leash, stroller, carrier, muzzle, or courtesy belt
- Seating rule: allowed on seat or must remain on floor
- Number-of-dogs limit, where applicable
- Amenities: water bowl, pet menu, waste bins, shade, and parking
- Last verified date and information source:
  - Venue confirmed
  - Community confirmed
  - User reported / needs reconfirmation

Primary actions:

- Navigate
- Call
- Save
- Share

## MVP: Trust and Community Updates

Keep contributions small and structured so that the venue policy remains reliable.

- Confirm that a policy is still correct.
- Report incorrect information.
- Add a missing condition.
- Add a recent visit photo or note.
- Mark a venue as permanently closed or temporarily unavailable.

Official policy data remains structured. User experiences provide supporting context rather than replacing it.

## MVP: Saved Places

Users can save venues into personal lists:

- Want to visit
- Favorites
- Good for rainy days
- Good for large dogs
- Weekend walk route

Saved places encourage return usage before Snoots! has a larger social network.

## Personalization

A lightweight dog profile improves results without expanding the MVP’s scope. It can personalize or filter venues based on dog size and relevant access needs, such as carrier or stroller requirements.

## Maps-First Navigation

1. **Explore** — map, search, filters, and venue details
2. **Saved** — personal venue collections
3. **Profile** — dog details and contribution history

## Deferred Modules

- **Playdate Matching:** initially use Maps to recommend a suitable meeting place; build matching later.
- **Social Feed:** begin with venue updates and visit notes as the early content layer.
- **Emergency Guidance:** later reuse Maps location, open-now status, and trust data, but provide a dedicated rapid emergency flow.

## Strategic Outcome

Maps delivers immediate utility on day one while building the verified location and policy data that can support every future Snoots! module.
