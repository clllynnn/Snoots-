User Story: As a millennial pet parent living in a dense Taiwanese city,I want to access highly verified behavioral transparency, upfront spatial rules, and immediate in-transit emergency guidance within a social network,So that I can confidently build an active lifestyle for my pet without the fear of fatal park violence, social rejection, or helpless medical panic.
Problem Statement: Amber is a modern, highly protective millennial pet parent living in a high-density Taiwanese city who views her dog as an interconnected part of her daily life who needs a single, verified ecosystem that provides upfront micro-level restaurant rules, verified behavioral histories of playmates, and immediate in-transit medical triage guidance during late-night emergencies because she views her pet as her child and wants to eliminate the constant baseline anxiety of navigating a chaotic urban environment filled with deceptive "fake" pet-friendly venues, unpredictable fatal park violence, and terrifying cognitive blind spots during medical crises.
Core Engine (Social - Highest Priority): A casual vertical feed integrating image sharing and forums, paired with a distinct "Tinder-style" playdate matching screen.
KPI: Profile Verification Depth & Community Trust Score (measuring owner accountability and dog behavioral declarations to prevent fatal attacks).
Secondary Feature (Midnight Healthcare): A stress-free emergency UI to guide panicked owners before arriving at the clinic.
KPI: Triage Engagement Rate & User Confidence Score (measuring helpfulness during the helpless vehicle transit window).
Tertiary Feature (Pet-Friendly Dining): A mapping layer breaking down "fake pet-friendly" marketing traps.
KPI: Accurate Expectation Rate (verifying micro-rules like stroller, seat, and size limits before departure).
